# Privacy Policy for Flip

**Effective Date:** November 15, 2026

Flip ("we", "our", or "the application") is built with a local-first philosophy. Your study data belongs to you and stays on your Windows PC.

## 1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
Flip does not require an account, login, or cloud synchronization. All personal study content—including your imported Excel (`.xlsx`) timetables, weekly tasks, deadlines, timer sessions, and app preferences—is stored strictly locally on your computer. We cannot access, view, or retrieve this data.

## 2. Optional Anonymous Usage Sharing (Telemetry)
Flip includes an optional anonymous usage sharing feature that is **disabled (off) by default**.
* **If left off:** Flip sends zero data over the internet.
* **If you opt in:** Flip collects basic, non-personally identifiable usage metrics (such as app version, operating system version, and anonymous feature interaction counts) solely to help improve app stability and performance.
* **No Personal Content:** Even when enabled, Flip never collects or transmits your timetable contents, task names, file paths, IP addresses, or personally identifiable information.
* **Opt-Out Anytime:** You can turn off anonymous usage sharing at any time inside Flip's settings.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

## 4. Code Signing & Distribution
Official Windows installers for Flip are digitally signed via [SignPath Foundation](https://signpath.org) using [SignPath.io](https://signpath.io).

## 5. Open Source Transparency
Flip is open-source software. You can inspect the complete source code at any time on GitHub: https://github.com

## 6. Contact
If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/issues
